Create a brand-new, distinctive, extremely simple symbol rather than a generic compass or stock radar icon. One bold, precise white abstract radar/R glyph: a thick three-quarter circular stroke open at the lower right, a short diagonal sweep extending from the circle's center toward the lower-right opening, and a single small solid circular signal dot separated inside the upper-right part of the ring. The whole mark should read as a unique, coherent R-like radar symbol at a glance, with generous negative space and balanced optical weight. Center the symbol and use a generous 18 percent safety margin on every side. Full-bleed square background, no rounded outer app tile, no border, no drop shadow, no 3D, no scene or mockup, no lettering, no text, no tiny details. Crisp production logo edges, confident contemporary geometry, recognizable at 32 pixels. Square 1:1 composition.
